Frequently asked questions

A few things you may want to know.

Who can apply for funding?

Varsity Lend is designed for tertiary students in Botswana. Application eligibility will be assessed according to the platform's funding requirements.

How do I apply?

Create a student account, sign in and complete the funding application with the requested information.

How will I know my application status?

Your application status will be displayed in your dashboard once your account and application services are connected.

Can I submit more than one application?

Application limits and repeat application rules will be communicated through the platform.
